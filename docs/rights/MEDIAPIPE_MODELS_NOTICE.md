# MediaPipe model attribution and license evidence

The downloaded MediaPipe models are developed by Google. The official model cards identify their authors and explicitly state **Apache License, Version 2.0**. A full copy is retained in [Apache-2.0.txt](Apache-2.0.txt).

The application model manifest retains each exact versioned Google source URL, size and SHA-256. Model-card source URLs, downloaded-document SHA-256 and short licensing quotations are recorded in [public-source-evidence.json](../../cook/evidence/rights-r06/public-source-evidence.json). That evidence maps the Face Landmarker bundle to the separate BlazeFace short-range detector, Face Mesh V2 and Blendshape V2 cards; Hair Segmenter, Hand Tracking, Selfie Multiclass Segmentation and BlazePose GHUM have their own cards.

Retain upstream notices and the license when distributing model files or derivatives. Document changes and do not imply Google endorsement. This notice does not license an upstream training dataset, portrait photography, product image, name/likeness right or other third-party material. It does not validate accuracy, fairness, clinical use or device performance.

Full model-card PDFs are research inputs; this notice retains short quotations and source receipts rather than rebundling their example photographs. Model/source-license documentation is a resolved audit item, not a declaration that every True Beauty distribution right is cleared. See [RIGHTS_AUDIT_R06.md](../RIGHTS_AUDIT_R06.md) for remaining blockers.
