## Assets, models, datasets

- Original UI mark, CSS and generic packaging/garment illustrations are new work. Product illustrations are explicitly not official packshots.
- Bundled demo photographs were downloaded from Unsplash image URLs; credit and use limits recorded in `docs/ASSET_PROVENANCE.md`. They are demo subjects, not a validation dataset or images of the user.
- DM Sans and DM Serif Display are self-hosted; separate SIL OFL notices are beside font files.
- Google MediaPipe models are separate downloads with source URLs and SHA-256 in `apps/web/public/models/manifest.json`; `packages/vision-core/MODELS.md` records unresolved model-specific distribution rights. Software license does not imply dataset/model clearance. No training is performed.
- `@techstark/opencv-js` carries Apache-2.0 and includes third-party libraries; dependency notices remain in installed package. No upstream facial mask raster assets are redistributed.
- Product catalogue is explicitly sample data with illustrative ingredient subsets and prices, official brand links, source/freshness fields; no live stock claims.

Publication/commercial model redistribution remains blocked until outstanding notices/model rights are resolved. Local evaluation is not a claim of legal clearance.
