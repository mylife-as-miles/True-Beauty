# Independent visual / UX critique — R07

Reviewed 2026-11-02 against the public hosted app at <https://true-beauty-miles.myles4miles.chatgpt.site/>. **Visual and editing-experience judgment: 8.3/10.** The art direction is coherent and the tested R07 repairs improve practical use. The requested luxury, immersive Mirror experience is not yet at 9. This is a bounded critic judgment, not the weighted full-product score, a scientific accuracy score, or device-performance certification.

## Concrete gaps blocking a 9

1. **Local material realism remains the largest visual gap.** At 85% intensity the enhanced plum lip is still read primarily as a uniformly tinted region. Gloss adds a concentrated lower-lip highlight; Glitter reads as sparse bright specks. The cheek treatment is a broad, soft color wash. The finish changes are real and useful, but these screenshots do not have the fine, varied surface response expected of a luxury cosmetic visualization. Improve edge falloff, preserved skin/lip texture and finish-specific highlight structure, then review matching source/finish/light crops. This is a visual judgment from a still demo portrait, not a claim of calibrated optical measurements. [Glitter](../stills/r07-independent-visual/desktop-enhanced-glitter.png), [gloss](../stills/r07-independent-visual/desktop-enhanced-gloss.png).

2. **The local garment preview looks conspicuously like a flat collage.** The brown procedural garment sits around the neck/raised hand as simple planes, with little fabric texture, drape or contact shading. The honest “Static photo composition” label helps, but the result does not share the Mirror's visual credibility. Keep its planning role clear and improve silhouette/overlap and material detail before treating it as an immersive try-on surface. A plausible generated blouse is a separate result. [Phone preview](../stills/r07-independent-visual/mobile-dressing-edit.png), [desktop Dressing](../stills/r07-independent-visual/desktop-dressing.png).

3. **The persistent Remix target is present but too small for precise visual correction.** The retained measurements show a 144px-wide image on desktop/tablet and 108px on phone. “Hair in focus” still displays the whole portrait, so the actual hair area is much smaller. At 1440px the preview card spans over 1100px while most of it is empty. This makes a purple tint perceptible, but makes edges, texture and placement hard to judge. Use a larger selected-region view or a resizable target alongside the inspector, while preserving the successful sticky behavior. [Fresh desktop](../stills/r07-independent-visual/desktop-remix-edit.png), [fresh phone](../stills/r07-independent-visual/mobile-remix-edit.png).

4. **Phone Focus mirror provides only a modest gain in immersion.** The title, history controls, look picker, source controls and editing panel still compete with the portrait. It is usable, but the focus state retains much of the ordinary page stack. A more decisive focus presentation and clearer on-canvas control hierarchy would better meet the intended experience. Preserve the visible exit and keyboard route. [Ordinary phone](../stills/r07-independent-visual/mobile-studio.png), [phone focus](../stills/r07-independent-visual/mobile-focus.png).

5. **Secondary spaces feel more form-led than editorial.** Dressing opens with a large title/intro/action block before the composition; Skin & care has another large heading followed by conventional forms. The type and colors are consistent, but the subject becomes secondary to page structure. Compact these introductions once the user is editing and bring the relevant portrait/result and active tool into the first viewport. This is a composition opportunity, not a reproduced overflow or broken-control defect. [Dressing](../stills/r07-independent-visual/desktop-dressing.png), [Skin & care](../stills/r07-independent-visual/desktop-skin-care.png).

## Evidence limits and next review

I did not inspect the reference products side by side and do not score visual similarity to Proofline or Common Table. The current branding reads coherent and original in the inspected frames. I did not test physical touch devices, camera tracking on a moving person, screen-reader workflows, sustained thermal/performance behavior, representative complexion/hair accuracy, provider retention/billing, or all 219 product capabilities. The zero-error/overflow result covers only the listed sessions and states. Retained axe evidence is a bounded automated check, not an accessibility certification.

Prioritize the local cosmetic/garment result quality and a larger selected-region editing view next. Preserve the verified R07 hair hydration, shared scene, persistent context, honest provenance and practical touch controls. No full 9/10 gate or product-completion claim follows from this review.
