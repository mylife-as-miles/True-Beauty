Demo portrait must be stable and labelled. Preserve the original file under `cook/stills/rNN/` and record browser/version, platform, route/state and capture command in the round ledger. Screenshots demonstrate visible states, not live model accuracy or paid-provider success.

## Critic packets

Visual critic: TB01–TB06, TB10–TB16 desktop/phone pairs, original art-direction brief, no implementing agent scores. Inspect face/mask crops at full size and texture/contrast/state continuity.

Technical critic: exact source diff, contracts, test outputs, provider evidence distinctions, performance traces and privacy behavior. Require source integration proof from every mandatory source. Do not let visual quality substitute for an absent engine.

Product critic: A–J workflow recordings, feature matrix, available/unavailable capability states and a fresh-browser session. Identify dead ends, overstated labels, missing controls and cross-engine continuity failures.

Security/fairness: consent/deletion/API boundary tests, client bundle scan, retention logs, complexion/lighting fixtures and limitations. No claims about actual people/datasets without clearance.

## Motion/performance

Record user-triggered controls at stable frame intervals; inspect transition start/middle/end, reduced-motion equivalent and focus. Measure render/inference scheduling separately. No 30/60 FPS claim without hardware/browser/resolution and a captured trace. Source references did not include approved motion files; no frame-by-frame reference match is claimed.
